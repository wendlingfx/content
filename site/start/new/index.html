I don't have cTrader

7 steps

1

Create your Fusion Markets login

Sign up with your email and a password. Fusion Markets is the broker we trade with and it offers cTrader Copy.

Sign up at Fusion Markets
2

Open a live account

In the Client Hub choose account type Zero, platform cTrader and currency USD.

3

Verify your identity

Upload a photo ID and a proof of address. Fusion says most accounts are approved within an hour.

4

Fund the account

Deposit at least US$500. Fusion charges no deposit fee on cards, PayPal, Skrill and Neteller.

5

Open the strategy and press Start copying

Log in to cTrader, open the Wendling FX strategy and press the green Start copying button.

Open the Wendling FX strategy
6

Choose the amount and confirm

Pick your live account, type the amount to allocate (minimum US$500), check the fee (30% of new profit only) and confirm.

7

Set your loss limit

In the strategy menu choose Set equity stop loss and type the lowest amount you accept. If your copy account reaches it, copying stops.

Fusion Markets does not accept clients from a few countries, including the United States, Japan, Spain and New Zealand. If that is your case, open a cTrader account with another broker that offers cTrader Copy (for example IC Markets or Pepperstone) and follow the same steps.

Done. Trades are now copied automatically, sized to your account. You can add or remove funds, change the loss limit or stop copying at any time.
