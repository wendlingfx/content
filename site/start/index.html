How to start copying

Copying runs inside cTrader Copy. Your money stays in an account in your name at a regulated broker. Which one is you?

1
I don't have cTrader7 steps. Open an account, verify, fund and copy.
2
I have cTrader with Copy5 steps. Your broker already offers cTrader Copy.
3
My broker has cTrader, no Copy7 steps. Add a Fusion account to the same login.

Not sure? If you have never opened cTrader, pick 1. If cTrader shows a Copy item in the left menu, pick 2. If it doesn't, pick 3.
