How to start copying

Copying runs inside cTrader Copy, on cTrader's own servers. Your money stays in an account in your name at a regulated broker. Pick your situation:

I don't have cTraderOpen an account at the broker we use, verify and fund it. I have cTrader with CopyYour broker already offers cTrader Copy. My broker has cTrader, no CopySome brokers offer cTrader without the Copy feature.

1. You don't have cTrader yet

  1. Open an account at Fusion Markets. Choose the Zero account, platform cTrader, currency USD. Fusion is the broker we trade with; it supports cTrader Copy and adds no extra fee to use it.
    Open a Fusion account
  2. Verify your identity in the Fusion Client Hub: a photo of your ID and a proof of address. This is required by the regulator.
  3. Fund the account. The minimum to copy this strategy is US$500. Fusion has no minimum deposit and no deposit fees on cards, PayPal, Skrill and Neteller.
  4. Log in to cTrader with the cTrader ID created with your account (web, desktop or the mobile app).
  5. Open the strategy and press Start copying. Choose your live account, type the amount to allocate, review the fees and confirm.
    Open the Wendling FX strategy
  6. Set your loss limit. In the strategy menu, choose Set equity stop loss and pick the lowest amount you accept for that copy account. If equity reaches it, copying stops and the copied positions are closed.

2. You already have cTrader with a broker that offers Copy

  1. Check your account type. Copying needs a live hedging account. If yours is netting, create a new hedging account with your broker under the same cTrader ID.
  2. Open Copy in the left menu of cTrader and search for Wendling FX, or use the link below while logged in.
    Open the Wendling FX strategy
  3. If the strategy appears, press Start copying, pick the account, allocate at least US$500, review the fees and confirm. Then set your equity stop loss (step 6 above).
  4. If it does not appear, your broker does not list this strategy. Follow path 3 below.

3. Your broker has cTrader but not cTrader Copy

Not every broker turns on the Copy feature. You can keep your current account and add a Fusion account to the same cTrader ID:

  1. Open a Fusion Markets Zero account with platform cTrader, using the same email as your cTrader ID, so the new account can be linked to the same login.
    Open a Fusion account
  2. Verify and fund it (minimum to copy: US$500).
  3. Follow steps 4 to 6 of path 1.

What happens after you start

The amount you allocate moves to a separate copy account in your name, used only for this strategy. Trades are sized in proportion to your equity versus ours, so your results move by about the same percentage as the account on this site.

If the strategy has positions open when you start, they are opened in your copy account at the current market price. Everything after that is copied as it happens.

Fees: 30% of new profit only, charged when your copy account reaches a new high. No fee when it doesn't. You can add or remove funds, change the loss limit or stop copying at any time from the strategy menu.

Common questions

Do I need to keep my computer on? No. Copying runs on cTrader's servers.

Why did a trade not copy? Usually because the copy account's leverage is lower than ours or its free margin is too small. Using a higher allocation or a higher leverage on the Fusion account avoids it.

Can I copy with a demo account? Demo accounts can only copy free strategies, so a live account is needed here.
