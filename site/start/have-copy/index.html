I have cTrader with Copy

5 steps

1

Check your account type

cTrader Copy works with hedging accounts. If yours is netting, open a new hedging account with your broker under the same cTrader login.

2

Find Wendling FX

In cTrader, open Copy in the left menu and search for Wendling FX. Or use the button while logged in.

Open the Wendling FX strategy
3

Press Start copying

If the strategy shows up, press Start copying. If it does not, your broker does not list it: go to "My broker has cTrader, no Copy".

4

Choose the amount and confirm

Pick your live hedging account, type the amount to allocate (minimum US$500), check the fee (30% of new profit only) and confirm.

5

Set your loss limit

In the strategy menu choose Set equity stop loss and type the lowest amount you accept. If your copy account reaches it, copying stops.

Done. Trades are now copied automatically, sized to your account. You can add or remove funds, change the loss limit or stop copying at any time.
