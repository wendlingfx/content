How it works

Three steps. You stay in control.

02

Log in with the same account, open my strategy and choose how much to allocate, from $500 ($1,000 or more suggested). My trades are copied in proportion to that amount.

Why cTrader Copy

Built for copying. Not bolted on.

cTrader is a trading platform made by Spotware and offered by regulated brokers worldwide. Its copy service is part of the platform itself.

Runs on cTrader's servers

Copying happens on the platform's own servers. You don't need a computer, a VPS or an app left open.

Sized to your account

Each trade is sized by the ratio between your equity and mine, so $1,000 copies the same risk as $10,000, just smaller.

Your own loss limit

Set an equity stop loss. If your account drops to that level, copying stops and the copied positions close automatically.

Fee only on new highs

The fee uses a high-water mark: you never pay twice for the same profit, and nothing is charged while the account is below its peak.

No lock-in

Add or remove funds, change the loss limit or stop copying at any time. No minimum period.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Where is my money?

In your own Fusion Markets account, in your name. Wendling FX never receives, holds or moves your funds.

How much do I need to start?

The minimum to copy is $500. We suggest $1,000 or more and at least 3 months, but that is your call.

What do I pay?

30% of new profit, charged automatically by cTrader Copy. No profit, no fee. If your account is below its previous high, there is no fee until that high is passed again.

Do I need to trade or watch the screen?

No. Every trade is copied to your account automatically, sized in proportion to the amount you allocate.

Can I stop copying?

Yes, at any time, in cTrader Copy. Your money stays in your Fusion account and you can withdraw it whenever you want.

Are the results real?

Yes. The account is live and verified by Myfxbook (track record and trading privileges).

Where do I follow the trades?

On the Telegram channel, with the daily and weekly results and short videos.

Risk

Read this before you copy.

What to expect

  • Discretionary trading, mainly Gold (XAUUSD). Other pairs, indices and BTC only when the setup is there.
  • No martingale, no grid.
  • Losing weeks will happen, and drawdowns can be deep. Check the live drawdown on Myfxbook before you copy.
  • The track record started in November 2026. Short histories can look better or worse than the long run.

What I can't promise

  • Profit. Past results don't guarantee future returns.
  • That your result will match mine to the cent. Slippage, spreads and account size create small differences.
  • That you won't lose money. Only copy with money you can afford to lose.
